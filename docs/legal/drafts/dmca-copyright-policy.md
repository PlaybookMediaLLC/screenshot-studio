## Send a notice

Email [[DMCA AGENT EMAIL]], or write to [[DMCA AGENT]], [[ADDRESS]]. Include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
3. the URL of the material on Shots;
4. your contact details;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the owner, its
   agent or the law;
6. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the
   owner or authorized to act for the owner.

## What happens next

- We remove or disable the material promptly and tell the workspace that posted it.
- That workspace may send a **counter-notice** with:
  - its signature;
  - the material and its former location;
  - a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
  - its contact details;
  - consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for its address (or, if outside the US,
    any judicial district where we may be found).
- We forward the counter-notice to the complainant. We restore the material in 10 to 14
  business days unless the complainant tells us they have filed a lawsuit.
