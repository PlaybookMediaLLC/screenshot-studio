# Acceptable Use Policy — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** Not legal advice. Not published.

## Summary

Use Shots to market your own products honestly. Don't use it to break the law, spam people,
break the rules of the networks you publish to, or attack the Service. We may remove content or
suspend accounts that do.

**Effective:** [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]

## You may not use Shots to

1. **Break the law.** This includes consumer-protection, advertising, privacy, anti-spam
   (CAN-SPAM, PECR, CASL) and export laws.
2. **Deceive.**
   - Make false or misleading claims about a product, price, review, endorsement or person.
   - Impersonate anyone.
   - Post undisclosed paid endorsements.
   - Use AI-generated people, logos or screenshots to mislead.
3. **Infringe.** Use images, trademarks, screenshots or text you don't have the right to use.
4. **Spam.**
   - Send announcements to people who did not opt in.
   - Ignore unsubscribes.
   - Post at volumes or in patterns a platform treats as spam or inauthentic behaviour.
5. **Break platform rules.** Violate the terms or policies of X, LinkedIn, Meta, Google or any
   other platform you connect, including their automation and AI-disclosure rules.
6. **Harm people.** Publish content that is hateful, harassing, violent, sexually explicit,
   exploits children, or promotes self-harm. Publish anyone's private information without
   their consent.
7. **Abuse the Service.**
   - Probe, scan or test our systems without following our
     [disclosure policy](/security/disclosure).
   - Get around rate limits, quotas or access controls.
   - Share accounts or keys outside your organization.
   - Resell the Service without a written agreement.
8. **Abuse crawling and capture.**
   - Use URL capture, link preview or website reading on sites you have no right to access.
   - Impersonate a search engine or social-network crawler, except to test pages you control.
   - Overload a site.
9. **Abuse AI.**
   - Generate content that breaks this policy.
   - Try to extract other customers' data or our prompts.
   - Use outputs to build a competing model.

## What we do

- We may remove content, pause publishing, revoke API keys, or suspend an account to stop a
  violation or protect others.
- Where reasonable, we tell you first and give you a chance to fix the problem.
- Report abuse to [[ABUSE CONTACT]].
