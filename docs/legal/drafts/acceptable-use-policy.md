# Acceptable Use Policy (draft)

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** Not legal advice. Do not publish. The live Terms have four
> short use rules. This policy replaces them. Code baseline: commit `bad06b3e`.

## Summary

- Shots publishes to social networks and sends email for customers. Abuse can harm third parties
  and can get our platform apps suspended.
- This policy lists what you must not do with Shots.
- We can remove content, pause a channel, or suspend a workspace that breaks it.

## Proposed text

**Acceptable Use Policy**

Version `[[VERSION]]`. Effective `[[EFFECTIVE DATE]]`.

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

### 1. Illegal and harmful content

Do not use Shots to create, store, or publish content that:

1. breaks the law or encourages others to break it;
2. infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, or publicity rights;
3. sexually exploits or endangers children;
4. threatens, harasses, or incites violence against a person or a group;
5. contains malware or links to malware.

### 2. Deception

1. Do not impersonate a person, a company, or a product.
2. Do not create fake screenshots, fake reviews, fake testimonials, or fake social posts that could
   mislead people.
3. Do not alter a screenshot of another product to misrepresent what it does.
4. Do not hide that content is advertising when the law or a platform requires a label.
5. Do not use AI output to mislead people about who made it when the law or a platform requires a
   disclosure.

### 3. Social networks

1. Follow the terms, rules, and automation policies of each network that you connect.
2. Connect only accounts that you own or have permission to manage.
3. Do not use Shots for spam, fake engagement, coordinated inauthentic behavior, or to avoid a
   network's limits or bans.

### 4. Email

1. Add an address to an audience list only if you have a lawful basis to email it.
2. Do not buy, rent, or scrape email lists.
3. Do not remove or hide the unsubscribe link.
4. Follow the CAN-SPAM Act and every other email law that applies to you and your recipients.

### 5. Websites and capture

1. Capture or read only public web pages, or pages that you have permission to use.
2. Do not use URL capture, link preview, or site reading to scrape sites at scale, or against a
   site's terms or `robots.txt`.
3. Do not try to reach private networks or internal addresses through Shots.

### 6. Security and the service

1. Do not probe, scan, or test the security of Shots without following our
   [Responsible Disclosure policy](responsible-disclosure.md).
2. Do not access another workspace, account, or key without permission.
3. Do not overload the service, go around rate limits or quotas, or share an account to avoid seat
   limits.
4. Do not resell or white-label Shots without a written agreement.
5. Do not use Shots to build a competing product from its outputs, prompts, or data. `[[COUNSEL: confirm]]`

### 7. Sensitive data

Do not upload sensitive personal information (for example health data or government ID numbers),
payment card numbers, or credentials of others, unless the law allows it.

### 8. Enforcement

1. We can remove content, pause a social connection, stop an email, revoke an API key, or suspend a
   workspace that breaks this policy.
2. We tell you what we did and why, unless the law or safety prevents it.
3. You can ask us to review a decision at `[[SUPPORT EMAIL]]`.

### 9. Report abuse

Report abuse to `[[ABUSE EMAIL]]`.
