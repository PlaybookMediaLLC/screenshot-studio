# Copyright and DMCA Policy (draft)

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** Not legal advice. Do not publish. Shots has no copyright
> policy today. Code baseline: commit `bad06b3e`.

## Summary

- Shots hosts customer files, and public share links can show them to anyone.
- This policy tells rights holders how to send a notice, and tells customers how to send a counter
  notice.
- To use the DMCA safe harbor, the company must register an agent with the US Copyright Office.

## Owner actions

1. Register a DMCA agent at the US Copyright Office and fill `[[DMCA AGENT]]`.
2. Set up `[[LEGAL EMAIL]]` and decide who handles notices.
3. Decide the repeat-infringer rule (`[[REPEAT INFRINGER RULE]]`).

---

## Proposed text

**Copyright and DMCA Policy**

Version `[[VERSION]]`. Effective `[[EFFECTIVE DATE]]`.

### 1. Our approach

We respect intellectual property rights. We respond to clear notices of copyright infringement in
content that Shots hosts, including public share links.

### 2. Send a notice

Send a written notice to our agent:

`[[DMCA AGENT]]`, `[[ADDRESS]]`, `[[LEGAL EMAIL]]`

The notice must include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. a description of the work that you say is infringed;
3. the address (URL) of the content that you say infringes;
4. your name, address, phone number, and email address;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized;
6. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or
   are authorized to act for the owner.

### 3. What we do

1. We remove or disable the content, or we tell you why we cannot.
2. We tell the workspace that posted the content and send it a copy of the notice.
3. We cannot remove posts that a customer published to a social network. Contact that network.

### 4. Send a counter notice

If you think that we removed your content by mistake, send our agent a counter notice with:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. a description of the content and where it was before removal;
3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith that we removed it by
   mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address, and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of
   `[[COURT FOR COUNTER NOTICES]]` and accept service from the person who sent the notice.

We can restore the content in 10 to 14 business days unless the rights holder tells us that it
filed a court action.

### 5. Repeat infringers

We end the accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate cases. `[[REPEAT INFRINGER RULE]]`

### 6. Other rights

For trademark, privacy, or other complaints, email `[[LEGAL EMAIL]]`.
